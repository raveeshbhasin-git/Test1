02 · What precedes the move

Three findings, one honest caveat

1

The perfect entry day looks weak — and is unknowable in real time

On the day before the optimal entry, the median breakout stock sat 29% below its 52-week high, below its 50-day average, RSI ≈ 38, with a negative 3-month return. The mathematically best entry is a local low — it only looks obvious in hindsight. Anyone claiming they can time the exact bottom of the base is selling something.

2

The tradeable tell is the ignition — the first violent week

What is catchable: the moment the move breaks cover. A +12% week on ≥1.5× normal volume, inside an intact uptrend (50-day MA above 200-day) marked the start of the discoverable portion of most great runs — Dell's Dec-2023 earnings gap, Micron's Apr-2025 surge, ANF's Aug-2026 report. Explosive moves start explosively. Strength begets strength.

3

Quiet breakouts don't work; catalysts do

The classic "tight base breaking to a new high on low noise" — with no volume shock — performed worse than baseline (2.9% vs 4.6% odds of +40%). Volume is the load-bearing ingredient: it's the footprint of institutions repositioning. And nearly every big episode maps to a nameable catalyst: an earnings inflection, a product/AI cycle, index inclusion, M&A, or a sector supercycle. The chart finds the footprint; the news tells you whether it's real.

The signal · backtested rule
IGNITION = 5-day return > +12%  AND  21-day volume > 1.5× its 126-day average  AND  50-DMA > 200-DMA

Universe: S&P 500 + S&P 400, price > $3, avg dollar volume > $5M. Evaluated on every qualifying ticker-day 2016–2026 (2.18M observations, 2,271 fires).

04 · The playbook

Six checks before acting on a fire

  • The jump: ≥ +12% in 5 sessions on ≥ 1.5× normal volume — the ignition itself.
  • The trend: 50-day MA above 200-day. Ignitions inside downtrends are usually short squeezes that fail.
  • The catalyst: a nameable reason — earnings inflection, product cycle, index inclusion, M&A, sector supercycle. No catalyst, no trade.
  • Relative strength: beating the market over 3 months, and improving.
  • Group confirmation: peers moving too (refiners 2026, AI servers 2023–24, life-science tools now) marks a theme, not a fluke.
  • The sizing: enter in tranches, expect the 1-in-4 shakeout of −20%, give it up to 3 months.

06 · Keep it running

How to track this going forward

The scan is three scripts in the repo (momentum/pipeline on branch claude/stock-momentum-patterns-op28f6). Re-run weekly — fires are rare (~4/week across 900 stocks) and stay actionable for weeks by design.

pip install yfinance pandas numpy requests lxml
export MOMENTUM_DATA=./data
python pipeline/download_data.py   # refresh 11y of daily bars (~2 min)
python pipeline/analyze.py         # episodes + backtest + today's screen
python pipeline/recent_fires.py    # ⚡ the live watchlist feed

Manual check between runs, for any stock: did it jump ≥12% this week? Is volume ≥1.5× normal? Is the 50-day above the 200-day? Is there a real catalyst? That's the whole signal — it fits on an index card.

Research artifact, not investment advice. Backtests are hypothetical, exclude costs/slippage/survivorship nuances (universe = current index membership), and past patterns — however robust across 11 years — do not guarantee future results. The ignition signal's edge comes with materially elevated drawdown risk (23% chance of −20% within the window). Position sizing is the only free lunch here.
